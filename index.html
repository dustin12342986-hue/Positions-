Voice guide

Talks you through each position, gives pace and breathing cues that follow the song, warns you before a change, and checks in now and then. Uses your device's own voices.

Settings

Say "Tempo stay", "Tempo next", "Tempo go back", "Tempo slower", "Tempo faster", "Tempo follow the music", "Tempo pause" or "Tempo play". Starting with "Tempo" keeps song lyrics from setting off commands. Voice runs through your browser's speech service (Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari).

Match your photos and videos

Pick as many files as you want. Each one shows a preview and a menu, so you choose which position it goes with. File names don't matter.

Add images, GIFs and videos in bulk

Each file is matched by name, English or Sanskrit, for example lotus.gif, indrani.mp4 or sammukha.png. Common names like cowgirl or doggy also work. Videos play muted on a loop.
